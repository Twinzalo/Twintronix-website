### Adding Your Own Custom Photos
If you have real photos of your own Twintronix installations, coldrooms, and team in Germiston/Gauteng:
1. Place your JPEG/PNG files in this `public/images/` directory.
2. For example, replace or add:
   - `public/images/hero-bg.jpg` (Hero banner)
   - `public/images/coldroom.jpg` (Freezer & coldrooms)
   - `public/images/ac-install.jpg` (Air conditioning installations)
   - `public/images/commercial-fridge.jpg` (Display coolers & under bars)
3. You can point any component to your `/images/<filename>.jpg` path whenever you want to showcase your real company photos.
